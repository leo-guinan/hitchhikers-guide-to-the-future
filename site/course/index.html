    Step 03 / name the mechanism

    Compression makes motion cheap—and meaning scarce.

    Solana memecoins are used here as a compression example: fast, cheap coordination can move attention quickly, but speed does not create trust or durable value by itself. Write down the mechanism you think could convert attention into valuable work. If you cannot name the conversion, you have a mood, not an investment thesis.

    Step 05 / compare the thesis

    The proposed mincoin is a hypothesis, not a security.

    The supplied structure contrasts a memecoin with a “mincoin”: a proposed system that records energy entering a capability, reports what that energy enabled over a period, and tests whether useful work compounds. The market page explicitly says its prototype records intent, does not accept money, and does not create a security. Keep those boundaries intact.
