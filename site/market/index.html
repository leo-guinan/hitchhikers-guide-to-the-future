WORK RECEIPT / PREPAID ENERGY

Do the work before the money arrives.

A work receipt records action taken on belief: what was promised, what was delivered, and what evidence exists. “Prepaid energy” is an internal accounting category for accepted work; it is not cash, equity, a guaranteed wage, or a debt until separate terms and settlement exist.

THE LINE

Receipts are not contracts.

Accepted work may later become eligible for payment under published terms. Until a separate agreement and settlement receipt exist, the ledger records energy contributed and a promise/result pair—not money owed. Investment execution, token issuance, custody, returns, redemption, and pooled matching remain disabled.

A receipt makes trust inspectable. It does not create trust by typography.
