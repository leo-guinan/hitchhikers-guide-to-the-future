The conditional promise

What this may become

If the product launches successfully and a pre-seed raise happens, we intend to work out a legally valid mechanism under which this early support could become a stock interest and a dividend participation. That mechanism does not exist yet. Nothing here is stock, a dividend, a guaranteed return, or a completed securities offering.

If that future does not materialize, the contribution becomes part of the story of belief and a promise of future work when Leo can be helpful. That promise is intentionally broad: maximum useful help for any amount, not a specific service package.

We are still figuring out the legal mechanism, eligibility, documentation, tax treatment, and custody/settlement rules. Do not contribute money you cannot afford to lose.

The cap is a boundary

When $50,000 is verified, this page ends.

The verified funding surface stops at the cap. No overflow round. No quiet extension. The final state is a small receipt saying the experiment is closed.

MINCOIN is an exploratory funding page, not legal, tax, investment, or financial advice. The intended future mechanism is unresolved and must be reviewed before any ownership or dividend claim is made.
