THE NARRATIVE

A market that pays to find the audience, not a machine that promises a payout.

Early readers are not a vanity metric. They are evidence about which people recognize the work, which framing converts, and what makes a reader stay. The market is useful only if it improves those observations.

Discover

Find a reader archetype, a community, a connection, or a message that the Guide would otherwise miss.

Test

Turn the hypothesis into a consequence-bearing experiment. A theory earns weight by changing the observable path.

Freeze

At cohort 1's end, freeze the evidence. Later growth belongs to a later market. The past should not acquire infinite obligations.

THE RECEIPT

What this page refuses to hide.

Boundary: this prototype does not accept funds, issue tokens, custody assets, promise returns, or create a revenue claim. A tradable instrument tied to subscriber growth and the efforts of others may be a security or another regulated product. Real purchases require a reviewed legal structure, eligibility and disclosure rules, custody/chain operations, tax treatment, and an explicit settlement oracle. The lock-intent form stores only a local browser receipt.
